## B01 · AI Adoption KPI

In alignment with the company’s AI Adoption requirement, I will drive AI adoption from three key areas: AI tool usage, AI business innovation, and AI capability enhancement, with a focus on frontend engineering scenarios.

### AI Tool Usage

Continuously use company-approved or compliant AI tools in daily frontend development and project delivery, including requirement understanding, UI solution design, component development, code generation and refactoring, bug analysis, testing, API integration, technical documentation, translation, and reporting. The goal is to improve development efficiency, code quality, issue diagnosis speed, and delivery stability, while building reusable prompts, checklists, and work templates.

### AI Business Innovation

Explore how AI capabilities can be integrated into frontend products and user workflows to improve user experience, operational efficiency, intelligent interaction, data presentation, and platform capability. Complete no fewer than three AI innovation directions throughout the year through research, solution design, prototype validation, or implementation, and further explore additional high-value AI scenarios where feasible.

### AI Capability Enhancement

Continuously enhance AI-related capabilities as a frontend engineer, including prompt engineering, AI-assisted coding, AI-supported UI/UX analysis, AI code review, AI-generated test cases, AI API integration, AI risk identification, and result validation. In addition, proactively develop practical AI development capabilities, including AI application fundamentals, model/API invocation, frontend-to-AI service integration, and AI feature prototyping.

## B02 · AI Governance Support: AI Governance system process design (Co-work with CIO office together)

In alignment with the AI Governance Support theme, as a business frontend developer, I will support the CIO Office, including the group IT and compliance-related teams, in the process design and system implementation of the company-wide AI usage governance framework. The objective is to standardize the use of AI tools and large model capabilities, reduce security and compliance risks related to source code, business data, sensitive information, non-compliant prompts, and unauthorized model usage, and support safe, compliant, controlled, and traceable AI adoption across the company.

Within the scope of my role, I will contribute mainly from the perspective of frontend system workflows and user experience. This includes supporting pages and interactions related to AI tool usage requests, AI use case registration, approval entry points, permission display, risk reminders, compliance confirmation, operation records, status tracking, and data presentation. By collaborating with the CIO Office and my team, I will participate in requirement understanding, process mapping, prototype or solution discussions, frontend feature development, page optimization, issue fixing, and necessary documentation.

The objective is to support AI Governance process implementation from the perspective of a frontend developer, helping translate AI usage control requirements into clear, usable, and traceable system workflows, while improving delivery quality, security and compliance, and user operability of the related system capabilities.

As a frontend developer, I will contribute from the perspective of frontend product experience, functional modularization, page interaction, asset management, 3D preview, workflow configurability, and delivery reusability, helping identify how digital avatar capabilities can be transformed from project delivery outputs into reusable product capabilities.
